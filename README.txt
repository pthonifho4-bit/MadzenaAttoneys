MADZENA ATTORNEYS WEBSITE
===========================

Files:
- index.html
- assets/madzena-attorney.jpg

Business email:
The website uses info@madzenaattorneys.co.za as the recommended professional business email.
This mailbox/domain must be registered and configured separately before it can receive mail.

How to use:
1. Open index.html in a browser to preview the site.
2. Upload index.html and the assets folder to your hosting account.
3. Point madzenaattorneys.co.za to the hosting account.
4. Create/configure info@madzenaattorneys.co.za with your domain email provider.
5. For true server-side enquiry processing, replace the mailto form handler with your preferred secure form/email backend.

The supplied image is used in the hero section.
